# Is Inkling 2 Mini an option for Cáptalo?

**Recommendation, 2026-11-21.** Run one offline, six-passage trial of the released Inkling 2 Mini as an optional Explain engine. Our M5 Ultra has 96 GiB of unified memory, comfortably above the publisher’s 32 GB Mac recommendation. The reason to try it is that a listener could get a tailored explanation without an API key, per-request charge or disclosure of the passage to a provider. Keep Explain on hold until a candidate demonstrates that its spoken words preserve the source. This assessment covers documentation and repository evidence. Inkling quality, peak memory and speech latency on this Mac are unmeasured.

## What the four-post announcement establishes

Thinking Machines announced the model, described the architecture, reported a monitoring experiment, then named local and hosted runtimes. The release has 26.4 billion parameters. Each token uses 3.4 billion distinct parameters, with about 5.4 billion effective activations because the middle twelve layers run three times. It accepts images and text and emits text. The authors demonstrated offline execution on a MacBook Pro. They report 20.1 on the Artificial Analysis Index and 92.8% on OmniDocBench v1.6. The latter is evidence of document perception. Neither score measures whether a spoken paraphrase preserves who did what or whether a marked region is read verbatim. [Announcement](https://x.com/thinkymachines/status/2123871643797995872) · [Architecture](https://x.com/thinkymachines/status/2123871645039464643) · [Monitoring](https://x.com/thinkymachines/status/2123871646176166358) · [Availability](https://x.com/thinkymachines/status/2123871647371501591) · [Release report and benchmark table](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/)

The researchers varied the pass count on a separately trained checkpoint. In their experiments, extra passes improved some reasoning tasks, shortened the written reasoning and showed no clear deterioration in their monitoring measures at this scale. Goodfire traced how information moves between layers. Neither investigation tested whether a generated explanation covers every essential point in its source. Use the fixed three-pass release for a product trial. Reducing its reasoning-effort setting can still leave internal reasoning before the final answer. Changing the loop count would require testing a different model. [Release model card](https://thinkingmachines.ai/model-card/inkling-2-mini/) · [Research model card](https://thinkingmachines.ai/model-card/tml-d24-varloop-research/) · [Goodfire study](https://www.goodfire.com/research/interpreting-inkling-2-mini/)

## What would fit the app

| Action | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Explain selected text or a region | Best fit. Pass the captured text or the existing OCR result to Inkling. Only the final explanation would go to the local voice. This needs no tools, browsing, screenshot retention or model-provider account. |
| Check another engine’s explanation | A separate possibility. Test it on faithful and altered answer pairs, including omissions, before putting a second inference on the time-critical speech path. A successful generator trial would leave verifier accuracy open. |
| Read Selection and Read Region | Keep the current reader. Exact selection already supplies the words. Vision recognizes marked regions. Generative transcription can change the text, and the release offers no measured improvement on Cáptalo’s region fixtures. |
| Speak in a built-in or personal voice | Inkling generates text. Voice synthesis and playback still require the existing speech engine or a dedicated voice model. |

The integration baseline registers both Read shortcuts and prepares the voice at launch. Vision operates on the supplied image and the speech service owns Kokoro playback. ## Hardware, runtime and distribution

The original main weight file is 19.53 GB at revision `ed40e7da625f594fc9c4990f5f66be24873c22d7`. Ollama lists a 20 GB local package. Hugging Face documents Python MLX inference with the original mixed-precision weights and support in llama.cpp. On this Studio, I would start with the original weights to avoid attributing a conversion error to the model. A supported external runtime is sufficient for a trial. Shipping inside the menu-bar app still requires a compatible native runtime, an explicit model download, cancellation and a check for unintended connections and retained text. Python MLX support alone does not establish Swift integration on macOS 26. [Pinned original files](https://huggingface.co/thinkingmachines/Inkling-2-Mini/tree/ed40e7da625f594fc9c4990f5f66be24873c22d7) · [Inference support](https://huggingface.co/blog/inkling2mini#inference-support) · [Ollama package](https://ollama.com/library/inkling-2-mini)

Unsloth, replying to the announcement, offers smaller GGUF conversions for 16 GB machines. Its guide puts 3-bit at 13–15 GB and its recommended 4-bit file at 17.6 GB. macOS, the source application, voice synthesis, context and temporary buffers also need memory. That makes a smaller conversion a separate quality and performance candidate. Success on the Studio would justify a trial on a lower-memory Mac before promising support there. [Unsloth reply](https://x.com/UnslothAI/status/2123876913886494853) · [Runtime and memory guide](https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/inkling-2-mini)

The publisher labels the weights Apache 2.0 and separately applies its Model Acceptable Use Policy to downloaded and modified materials. The policy addresses downstream use and disclosure of material limitations. Evaluate those obligations for the intended distribution and learner audience before exposing the feature. Local inference removes the cloud credential and provider-retention questions for this route. [Model card](https://thinkingmachines.ai/model-card/inkling-2-mini/) · [Model Acceptable Use Policy](https://thinkingmachines.ai/model-acceptable-use-policy/)

## What would change the recommendation

1. **Test the existing six passages first.** Freeze the release revision, runtime, chat template, reasoning effort and output limit. Run text-only with synthetic inputs and no network after setup. Score the final answer blind for factual support, required facts, depth, length and style. In the earlier pilots, each of Terra and Sol passed four of six. One answer changed approval to paint a door into a claim about what would happen. Another omitted what an archive label meant. One genuine miss ends the pilot. Six passes earn the full 83-case adult qualification, including held-out cases and 3,000-word coverage. [Recorded pilot outcomes](../plans/2026-11-21-explain-qualification-recovery-notes.md#L62) · [Review rubric](../../Tests/ExplainQualification/RUBRIC.md#L5)
2. **Measure how long the listener waits.** In the pilot, record cold load, time to first complete speakable sentence and peak memory with the voice ready. Exclude private reasoning from speech. A slow first sentence can rule the candidate out early. If the content passes, the product test is key-down to the first audible word in at most 3.0 seconds on every 30-, 200- and 300-word trial, including cold launch, both shortcuts and the region drag. Tokens per second cannot establish this result. [Native acceptance procedure](../plans/2026-11-20-explain-own-key-phases/phase-5.md#L11)
3. **Keep the evidence for a verifier separate.** If independently considered for checking answers, start with the four historical answers and the eight fresh faithful/faulty pairs. Apple’s verifier accepted seven of eight fresh faulty answers and took over three seconds on three calls. Inkling would need to preserve faithful paraphrases, catch unsupported claims and omissions, and leave enough time for generation and speech. [Verifier results and timing](2026-11-21-explain-offline-verifier.md#L35)

Earlier on-device generators also missed the quality bar. Apple Foundation Models had 33 confirmed faithful answers out of 70 and the tested Qwen had 16. Those results explain why a new model deserves a small blind trial before app work. They do not predict Inkling’s score. [Local engine comparison](2026-11-20-explain-my-way-engine-assessment.md#L48)
