# Listen on the Studio and qualify ordinary Macs later

Exercise the signed candidate from its installed app path. Record the app revision, signature, model and runtime revisions, hardware, macOS, speed, sample length and whether the helper is warm. Keep captured text and personal audio out of the evidence report.

**Automated acceptance on the Studio**

- Run the built-in reader on a fresh feature profile. Verify no Own Voice download, microphone prompt, helper process or model memory allocation.
- After enrollment, read synthetic selection and region fixtures with connectivity denied. Calibrate the blocking harness and observe attempts as well as successful connections. Include first launch after a restart. Verify targeted text and audio do not appear in logs or temporary files.
- Measure installed app size with and without the helper, explicit download bytes, installed model/profile storage, idle and peak combined memory, cold and warm shortcut-to-first-audible-output and elapsed synthesis time per second of audio. Separate output-device onset from the helper's first PCM. Measure Stop-to-silence against the reader's existing 150 ms target. Compare to a stock voice under the same workload.
- Record ten minutes of synthetic playback in the test harness. Compare phrase order and coverage against the source, inspect interruptions, speed drift and omitted endings, then ensure personal data is removed on deletion. Run make verify and retain privacy-safe evidence.

**Listening acceptance.** After the owner enrolls, ask them to listen to the full ten-minute passage and judge likeness and comfort at the end. Follow the source text to note skipped, repeated or cut-off words. Use a passage with multiple paragraphs, numbers, a file name, an acronym and a URL. Check pronunciation, audible pauses, accuracy and fatigue. A synthetic trial or automatic transcript cannot answer the likeness question. If it fails, record the failing passage category and keep the built-in voices available.

**Later efficiency round.** When feature work is finished, repeat resource and onset measurements on the owner's M4 laptop and Mac mini. Record actual memory configurations and compare with the Studio and built-in reader, including whether playback keeps pace and memory pressure occurs. Review those figures with the owner before claiming ordinary-Mac usability. This schedule follows the owner's November 22 decision.

**Work split.** Studio listening and the later two-machine qualification use the same candidate and measurement definitions. A later build must record what changed before reusing results.
