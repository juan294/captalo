# Phase 5 — Verify the candidate on the Mac and listen for a week

Requires [Phase 4](phase-4.md). Keep a receipt identifying source SHA, worktree, signed build, model/configuration/prompt digests, OS, output device, network class and fixture IDs. Separate observations for each exposed provider and audience. Any materially changed input invalidates the affected result.

## Automated acceptance

1. Run the complete sequential local `make verify` and preserve first failures. In addition to the existing finite reader, speech-only offline and render probes, require the new app-action tests, wire/egress checks, privacy canaries, prompt-fixture coverage, Keychain lifecycle and interruption/recovery tests. Ordinary verification uses synthetic keys and fixtures and can run without a live provider account. The integration owner alone performs the authorized fixed-path signed installation after the local gates.
2. On the installed app with prepared voice assets, exercise both Read shortcuts, including actual marked-region OCR with the network disabled. Use process-scoped connection-attempt evidence alongside a recording transport. Cover launch, panel open, local Save, voice preview, disconnected/ineligible Explain, empty or cancelled capture and offline failure. A blocked connection by itself cannot prove zero attempts. With an eligible key and explicitly authorized synthetic request, verify exactly one request to the expected destination and match the adapter contract probe. Check process-attributed egress and test payload structure without recording keys, passages or real traffic bodies. Test an interrupted stream with the fixture transport, including failure after the first audible sentence.
3. Use distinct synthetic source, answer, profile and credential canaries. Scope the inspection to application logs, preferences, app support/cache, HTTP cache, debug export and install record. Only the intentional synthetic profile may persist in its setting and only the credential may persist in its designated Keychain item. Inspect successful, failed and interrupted requests, relaunch and disconnect. No real content or secret belongs in the evidence. Exercise disconnect during a stream and verify a subsequent Explain needs a new connection.
4. Exercise selection from a real editor and Rex, plus an image/scanned-PDF region with known text inside and a distinct sentinel outside. Confirm only the inside text reaches the fixture adapter. Confirm unchanged clipboard types/data and restored source focus, a full 3,000-word source, correct distant exceptions, audible natural completion and exactly one over-limit sentence. Repeat the shorter cases on each candidate provider. Exercise eligible/blocked learner cases with simulated actors and synthetic material only.
5. Instrument real key-down, first chime sample, chime end, region release, first complete text, scheduled speech and output-device speech onset on a common timeline. Use a controlled short drag for reproducible region trials. For each provider and shortcut run 30-, 200- and 300-word cases once after cold launch with the local voice ready and twice warm. Record every elapsed time including misses, refused calls and outliers. Use a synthetic audio loopback or calibrated recording and inspect the waveform/clip to distinguish spoken onset from chime. The acceptance limit is **at most 3.0 seconds for every measured key-down to first spoken word**. Report physical drag duration and release-to-word separately. Do not subtract drag time, ignore a slow run or replace the maximum with a percentile. Verify immediate chime onset (target within 100 ms of key-down), 0.9–1.1 seconds of audible chime and no word before its end. Any miss holds that route for repair and a documented rerun or an owner decision to change the brief.

## Manual acceptance

- Juan listens blind to signed-candidate outputs, checks all three depth positions and all four styles, and verifies the two About me examples. Listen to early speech and the last exception in the long document. Hear the normal and interrupted ending. Check actual device switching, focus, menu-bar activity and stop/replacement. A first-time key holder connects using only the in-app instructions. Verify local key removal and provider-specific privacy copy. Do not store their credential or content in the report.
- Use the candidate on real email, documents and agent output for seven days only with the owner's explicit choice to send each passage to the connected provider. Record dates, route, helpfulness and chime comfort as content-free observations. On day seven record whether Juan still chooses Explain, judges it helpful and finds the chime pleasant. Do not introduce telemetry or keep source text, screenshots, answers or real audio. A declined, incomplete or negative trial remains visible in the disposition.
- For direct learner operation, verify the intended account, jurisdiction, guardian authorization and safeguards against the eligibility record before first use. Exercise synthetic eligible and ineligible flows. A child trial is unnecessary for the product gate. No unverified configuration appears as available to a learner.

## Exit and recovery

The integration owner keeps one accepted evidence record per exposed route. Native tests needing permissions, signed installation, external spend or human listening remain pending until performed within their scope. Repair a failed gate and rerun affected checks on the exact candidate. Omit an unqualified optional provider or learner route with a reason. An unavailable OpenAI candidate or a failed week-long product verdict returns to Juan. This phase supplies a local product verdict. Distribution follows the repository's separate release procedure.

One owner runs hardware and week-long acceptance sequentially. There is no independent execution unit sharing the key store, audio device, TCC permissions, installed app or clipboard. Record ownership of temporary synthetic recordings and test credentials beforehand and close only the verified task-owned resources.
