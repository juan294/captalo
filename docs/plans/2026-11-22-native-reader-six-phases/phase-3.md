# 3. Stop and replace the reading

During a sufficiently long synthetic passage, repeat the same selection to stop. Also try an empty selection while reading. Measure the last old speech after shortcut activation; the proposed limit is 150 ms. Start a fresh passage and switch to different selected words while it plays. Compare with isolated playback of the new words when available and inspect the app’s playback events. Record stop/replace behavior per app, any overlapping or returning old speech, and gaps in observation. Results are local to the tested machine, build, voice and device.
