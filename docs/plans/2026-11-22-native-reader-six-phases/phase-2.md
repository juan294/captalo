# 2. Time the reader

Use a short synthetic sentence, the installed build and its configured shortcut. Record key-down and the first observed speech signal on the same monotonic clock. Label first read after restarting Cáptalo separately from a warm read. Record whether the app had completed its startup voice preparation. The proposed warm limit is one second. A scheduled buffer, a chime or a textual capture alone cannot establish first-word latency. Report output-route and speaker limitations.
